Files used, unmodified: svg/original/*.svg (following the repository's
symbolic links into svg/groups), svg/groups/shared/wait/*.svg and
svg/groups/original/left_ptr_watch/*.svg, copied to svg/.

curgen.py applies the Original Classic colors from the repository's
render.json (#00FF00 and #FF0000 to #000000, #0000FF to #FFFFFF), renders each
256-pixel SVG at the art size Bibata uses for each Windows cursor size
(22 in 32, 27 in 40, 32 in 48, 38 in 56, 43 in 64, 48 in 72, 54 in 80, 64 in 96
and 86 in 128, from configs/normal/win_rg.build.toml), places it at the top
left of the cursor as clickgen does, and writes PNG frames into ../ocr_*.cur.
The 54-frame wait and left_ptr_watch animations go into ../ocr_wait.ani and
../ocr_appstarting.ani at 2 jiffies per frame (win_delay 1, doubled by
clickgen). Hotspots come from the same configuration, scaled and truncated as
clickgen does (128,128 when a cursor has none).
