Files used, unmodified: cursors/Breeze_Light/src/svg/*.svg, copied to svg/.

curgen.py renders the 32-pixel canvas of each SVG at 32, 40, 48, 56, 64, 72,
80, 96 and 128 pixels (sips on macOS, or rsvg-convert), and writes them as PNG
frames into ../ocr_*.cur. The 23-frame wait and progress animations go into
../ocr_wait.ani and ../ocr_appstarting.ani at 2 jiffies per frame, the closest
to Breeze's 30 ms. Each hotspot comes from the SVG's hotspot rectangle, rounded
down at each size as Breeze does.

    ocr_normal.cur, ocr_icon.cur, ocr_cdautostart.cur   default
    ocr_ibeam.cur                                       text
    ocr_wait.ani                                        wait-01..23
    ocr_cross.cur                                       crosshair
    ocr_up.cur                                          up-arrow
    ocr_sizenwse.cur                                    size_fdiag
    ocr_sizenesw.cur                                    size_bdiag
    ocr_sizewe.cur                                      size_hor
    ocr_sizens.cur                                      size_ver
    ocr_sizeall.cur, ocr_size.cur                       fleur
    ocr_no.cur                                          not-allowed
    ocr_appstarting.ani                                 progress-01..23
    ocr_help.cur                                        help
    ocr_pen.cur                                         pencil
    ocr_hand.cur                                        pointer

Regenerate with: python3 -I curgen.py svg ..
