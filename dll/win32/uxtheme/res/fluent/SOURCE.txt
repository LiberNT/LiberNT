Files used, at every native size of 12, 16, 20, 24 and 28 pixels that exists:
- assets/Dismiss/SVG/ic_fluent_dismiss_<size>_regular.svg (close)
- assets/Square/SVG/ic_fluent_square_<size>_regular.svg (maximize)
- assets/Square Multiple/SVG/ic_fluent_square_multiple_<size>_regular.svg (restore)
- assets/Subtract/SVG/ic_fluent_subtract_<size>_filled.svg (minimize)
- assets/Question/SVG/ic_fluent_question_<size>_regular.svg (help)

Each SVG is rasterized at its native size (sips -s format png on macOS, or any
SVG renderer), then capglyphs.py writes every frame with the renderer's alpha
unchanged and only the state color applied: w, i, d for dark captions and l, li,
ld for light captions.
